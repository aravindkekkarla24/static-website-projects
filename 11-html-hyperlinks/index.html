Virat Kohli is one of the most popular and successful cricketers from India. He is widely regarded as one of the finest batsmen of his generation. Kohli is known for his aggressive batting, excellent fitness, and determination. He has represented India in international cricket for many years. His passion for the game and consistency have earned him millions of fans. He is also admired for his leadership and competitive spirit.

Virat Kohli was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India. He developed a strong interest in cricket when he was very young. His father supported his cricketing ambitions and encouraged him to train regularly. Kohli joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy to develop his skills. He quickly became a talented player in Delhi's junior cricket system. His performances eventually earned him a place in India's Under-19 team.

Virat Kohli captained India to victory in the 2008 Under-19 Cricket World cup. He made his international debut for India in 2008. He gradually established himself as an important batsman in the Indian team. Kohli became successful in Tests, One Day Internationals, and T20 cricket. He also served as captain of the Indian cricket team for several years. His aggressive approach and consistent performances made him a global cricket star.

Kohli was a member of India's 2011 Cricket World Cup winning team. He has received several prestigious ICC awards during his career. He has been recognized multiple times as one of the world's leading cricketers. Kohli has also achieved major milestones while playing for India and in the IPL. His performances have helped India win important matches and series. He is considered one of the most successful Indian batsmen of the modern era.

Virat Kohli has scored thousands of runs in international cricket. He has scored more than 50 centuries in international cricket. He is among the highest run-scorers in men's One Day International cricket. Kohli has achieved centuries and major scoring milestones in different formats. He is particularly famous for his consistency while chasing targets in ODIs.
